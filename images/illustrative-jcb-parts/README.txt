Illustrative JCB parts reference images
=======================================

Source: User-supplied generated catalog-sheet crops and name-to-file manifest.
Use: Included for illustrative-reference display in this website with the user's
explicit approval.

These generated images are not guaranteed to depict an exact product, supplier
part, JCB model fitment, or OEM item. They do not verify specifications, brand,
availability, stock, price, or part-number claims. Every displayed image must
retain its "Illustrative reference only - not the exact product or an OEM photo"
caption.

Saved inventory uses only the exact component-name matches in
data/illustrative-jcb-parts.json; only a trailing "(Batch N)" suffix is ignored.
Similar, ambiguous, or unmatched part names intentionally receive no image.

No independent copyright license or supplier permission is asserted by this
note. Do not treat these illustrations as evidence of compatibility or as
verified product photography.
